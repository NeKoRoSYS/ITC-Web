Frequently Asked Questions

Do you provide billiard powder?

We stopped providing powder, and we lend billiard gloves for free instead.

Are outside food and drinks allowed?

Yes, you can bring your own food and non-alcoholic drinks upstairs.

Can we vape or smoke inside?

Vapes are allowed inside. Cigarettes are prohibited indoors, but there is a smoking area outside.

When is the next tournament?

The next VM-hosted tournament is planned for next year. Updates will be posted on our Facebook page.

House Rules

  1. 1. Clean As You Go

    Keep your table area tidy and dispose of trash properly.

  2. 2. No Alcohol

    Bringing or drinking alcoholic beverages inside the lounge is prohibited.

  3. 3. No Gambling

    Cards, dice, and other forms of gambling are prohibited upstairs.

  4. 4. Respect Everyone

    Be courteous to fellow players, staff, and lounge equipment.
